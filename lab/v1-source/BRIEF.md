## Score

| Beat | Device | Purpose |

| Approach | Flow + short entrance | Calm before the denser work surface |

Four families: parallax, entrance, pin, reveal. No scrub/video assets. Reduced motion removes pinning and displacement while retaining all controls and content. Phones use a compact cover, horizontal tab scroller with keyboard access, and a vertical diagram.
